- **Manager permissions are modelled as absence, not disablement**: no submit control rendered at all, and the update action rejects `manager` server-side. Client and server agree.
- **The ADR promotion is complete** — new ADR file, index rows, and the `MODIFIED` entry applied to the atomic-writes ADR with its `Touching` row correctly left alone.
