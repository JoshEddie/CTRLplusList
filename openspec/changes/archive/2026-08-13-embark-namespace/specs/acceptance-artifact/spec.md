## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: acceptance.md is a schema-registered artifact drafted by chaining sourced scenarios

The project-local schema fork at `openspec/schemas/spec-driven-review/schema.yaml` SHALL register an `acceptance` artifact that `generates: acceptance.md` with `requires: [specs]`, driven by an artifact template (`openspec/schemas/spec-driven-review/templates/acceptance.md`); the content contract (format, lifecycle, exclusions) lives in the artifact's schema instruction — the fork is repo-owned, so no `openspec/config.yaml` rules override is needed.

**Position** — the fork's artifact declaration order SHALL be `proposal, specs, design, acceptance, tasks, review`, because `/opsx:continue` selects the next artifact by declaration order. Acceptance SHALL sit after `design`, which is where step-to-step connection was reasoned about and is therefore both the better failure detector and the source of prose for a step; and before `tasks`, because the run repairs specs and tasks derive from specs. The `requires` edges SHALL NOT change: `acceptance` keeps `requires: [specs]` alone, since `design` is conditional and an edge onto it would leave acceptance reading `blocked` whenever design is skipped. Upstream's `proposal, specs, design, tasks` relative order SHALL be preserved so the fork stays reconcile-clean on `openspec update`.

**The draft** — generation SHALL draft user-journey flows by chaining the change's touched `#### Scenario:` blocks together with pre-existing links found in canonical `openspec/specs/**/spec.md` — the same grep-the-corpus discipline `rules.proposal` mandates. Every row SHALL be sourced: a step the corpus cannot back SHALL be surfaced as a finding, never written around on the flow's own authority.

**Not a qualification by itself** — generating this artifact is not a qualification run. `/embark-qualify` (`trunk-workflow`) is the layer that runs the generation in a context that did not author the documents and gates the change on the findings it surfaces. Generated by any other route the artifact is equally valid and drafted to the same contract; validating the specs and design it chains is then the owner's own job rather than a gate the artifact enforces. The contract below therefore states no verdict.

**design.md never sources behavior** — a step SHALL chain on a spec scenario or not at all. Where a spec backs the step but names no observable, design.md MAY supply the concrete surface the row asserts — the handle, not the behavior — and that is not a finding. Where design.md is the only thing asserting the behavior itself, or asserts behavior a spec denies, the step is not sourced and SHALL be surfaced.

**What a surfaced step is SHALL NOT be settled here** — classifying it, repairing it and gating on it belong to whoever ran the generation. `/embark-qualify` (`trunk-workflow`) carries the failure types, the repair route and the verdict; on any other route the owner judges the surfaced step. The artifact contract states which rows may be written, and stops there.

#### Scenario: Propose drafts acceptance flows from scenarios

- **WHEN** a change's `acceptance` artifact is generated after its delta specs exist
- **THEN** `openspec/changes/<name>/acceptance.md` exists with flows chaining the change's scenarios and any pre-existing cross-capability links from canonical specs

#### Scenario: Acceptance is generated after design and before tasks

- **WHEN** `/opsx:continue` runs on a change whose `specs` and `design` are complete
- **THEN** it selects `acceptance` as the next artifact, and `tasks` is generated only after the acceptance run and any repair have completed

#### Scenario: An unchainable journey surfaces a spec gap

- **WHEN** drafting a flow reveals a step no existing or delta scenario covers
- **THEN** the step is surfaced and the flow does not chain through it, rather than the flow inventing unspecified behavior on its own authority

#### Scenario: Design wording is used, design backing is not

- **WHEN** a delta scenario backs a step but names no observable, and design.md names the concrete surface
- **THEN** the row asserts design's observable and reports no finding; but where design.md is the only thing asserting the behavior, the step is surfaced rather than chained on design's authority

#### Scenario: The artifact does not classify what it surfaces

- **WHEN** the draft surfaces a step it cannot source
- **THEN** the artifact contract names no failure type, no repair route and no verdict for it — those belong to `/embark-qualify` under `trunk-workflow`, or to the owner on any other generation route

#### Scenario: Acceptance generated outside the qualify layer is still valid

- **WHEN** `acceptance.md` is generated by a route other than `/embark-qualify`
- **THEN** it is drafted to the same contract, and validating the specs and design it chains falls to the owner rather than to a gate the artifact enforces
