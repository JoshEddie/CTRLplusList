### What looks good

- The floor model is genuinely two-valued and centralized: `RoleFloor`/`FLOOR_ROLES`/`meetsFloor` in one module, every gated write naming its floor at the `authedWriter` call site rather than hand-rolling role comparisons.
- The ≥1-owner floor is a guarded single statement (`DELETE … WHERE EXISTS (another owner)`), honoring the no-interactive-transactions rule instead of a read-then-write race — and it earned its own ADR.
- `redeemInvite`'s single-statement CTE makes unknown, expired, and spent tokens indistinguishable to the caller by construction, not by branching.
- Unit coverage of the DAL is substantive: `profile.members.actions.test.ts` (565 lines) drives real pglite, asserts refusals *and* row state, and the block/expiry/double-redeem edges are all present.
- Four ADRs promoted with `INDEX.md` rows and the `DATABASE.md` cross-reference — the decision trail is complete.
- The disabled-not-absent affordance principle is stated, spec'd, and applied consistently within the Permissions section itself.
