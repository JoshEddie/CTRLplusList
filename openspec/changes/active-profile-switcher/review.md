### What looks good

- The self/active split is the right seam: `Actor` naming both profiles, `authedWriter` gating on membership, and `stampActedAs` recording recency each have one owner.
- Cookie handling is properly server-only — `httpOnly` + `sameSite` + `secure`, read through `next/headers`, never surfaced to the client bundle.
- Five ADRs recorded for the load-bearing decisions (block-belongs-to-the-human, selection-in-a-cookie, actor-resolution, env override, active-as-authorization-context), each indexed.
- `ProfileSwitchProvider` reads the path from `window.location` rather than `usePathname()` to avoid making the whole `(main)` tree a blocking prerender — a real constraint, correctly reasoned in task 14.4.
- The unsaved-changes hold on switching (`useUnsavedChanges` + `ConfirmDialog`), including release-on-unmount, is covered four ways in the provider suite.
- New e2e coverage (`profile-switch.auth.spec.ts` + the `activeProfile` helper) drives the switch through the real dropdown rather than stubbing it.
- The migration ships with its snapshot and journal entry, and `openspec validate --strict` passes.
